There's no definitive guide to using AI in your daily work. Instead, it starts with a question: how could AI make this easier for me and my team? Then you find out by trying it on your actual work, over and over, and noticing where it saves you an hour and where it reliably wastes one. You learn what context and tools it needs from you to do anything useful, and how to judge what it gives you back.

The catch is, that intuition has a shelf life. Something that failed in January might be easy now. So "I tried it once and it wasn't great" only tells you how it did that day under a certain set of conditions. (Model, prompt, data, context window, tools, connectors, mood.) Great for someone like me who enjoys tinkering, but difficult for someone who has a full-time job that isn't experimenting with AI.
