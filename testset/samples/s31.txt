I started using AI to take meeting notes three months ago. The results surprised me.

The notes themselves were fine. What changed was how I showed up in meetings. I stopped typing and started listening. I asked better questions. I noticed when someone went quiet.

It turns out the value wasn't in the transcript. It was in the attention I got back.

There are tradeoffs, of course. Some clients are uncomfortable being recorded, and the summaries sometimes miss the point of a discussion entirely. But for internal meetings, I'm not going back.
