Subject: Proposal: Wednesday No-Meetings Day

Hi Team,

I'd like to propose trying a no-meetings day every Wednesday for one month, starting December 2.

The goal is to give everyone more uninterrupted time to focus on important tasks, complete projects, and work through priorities without frequent calendar interruptions.

During the trial, let's avoid scheduling internal meetings on Wednesdays wherever possible. Of course, urgent operational matters and genuinely time-sensitive discussions can still take place when necessary.

At the end of the month, we'll gather feedback to see whether the change has improved productivity, reduced distractions, and helped us manage our workloads more effectively.

I'd appreciate hearing your thoughts and any concerns before we get started.

Thanks for your continued hard work and collaboration!

Best,
Jordan
Operations Manager
