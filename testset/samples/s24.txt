Welcome back to the newsletter. This month, we're looking at a question that comes up in almost every client conversation: how do you know if an AI project is working?

It sounds simple. In practice, it's anything but. Many teams track activity, such as logins and prompts sent, rather than outcomes. Others rely on anecdotes. Few have a clear baseline to compare against.

In this issue, we'll look at three practical ways to measure impact, share a short case study from a mid-sized logistics firm, and highlight a few tools worth watching.
