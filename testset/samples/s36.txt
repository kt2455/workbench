How to Give Feedback That Actually Helps Your Colleagues

Giving feedback is an essential part of working well with others, but knowing how to deliver it effectively isn't always easy. Whether you're addressing a missed deadline, recognizing excellent work, or suggesting a better approach, the way you communicate can make all the difference.

Useful feedback isn't about pointing out mistakes or telling someone what they're doing wrong. It's about helping colleagues understand their strengths, identify opportunities to improve, and feel confident about their next steps.

So, how can we make feedback more constructive and meaningful?

The key is to focus on specific examples, choose the right moment, and approach every conversation with respect. With a little preparation and empathy, feedback can become a powerful tool for building trust, improving performance, and creating a stronger workplace.
