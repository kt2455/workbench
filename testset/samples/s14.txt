We hired eleven people last year. I interviewed most of them, and I got at least three wrong.

Looking back, the pattern is obvious. I over-weighted confidence and under-weighted curiosity. The people who interviewed best weren't always the people who worked best.

So this year we changed the process. Every candidate now does a paid, two-hour working session with the team they'd join. No slides. No brainteasers. Just real work.

It's slower. It's more expensive. And it's the best hiring decision we've made.

Hiring isn't about finding the perfect candidate. It's about finding out the truth, quickly, for both sides.
