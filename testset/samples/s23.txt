Last week, our team wrapped up a six-month digital transformation project. Here's what I learned.

It wasn't about the technology. It was about the people.

When we started, I thought success meant shipping on time. I was wrong. Success meant building trust — with stakeholders, with end users, and with each other.

Three lessons that will stay with me:

1. Listen more than you speak. The best insights came from the quietest voices in the room.
2. Embrace the messy middle. Progress isn't linear, and that's okay.
3. Celebrate small wins. Momentum is everything.

I'm incredibly grateful to the talented, dedicated, and resilient team who made this possible. This is a testament to what's possible when passionate people come together around a shared vision.

Onward and upward. 🚀
