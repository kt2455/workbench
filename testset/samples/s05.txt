3. Leverage a virtual collaboration tool like Miro

Virtual whiteboarding tools have made huge improvements since being thrust into the limelight during the COVID-19 pandemic. We leverage tools like Miro with many of our clients to help facilitate engaging workshops, capture requirements, and unlock innovation. The features of the infinite virtual whiteboarding canvas help you capture some of the magic previously only possible when working in person. On top of the real-time collaboration these tools enable, they also provide access to a universe of templates and ideas for creative ways to effectively facilitate a variety of meetings (including design thinking techniques mentioned above).

Whatever your role may be in the corporate world, meetings are practically unavoidable, but with these tips and tools, you can become the meeting hero that saves your team from boring and unproductive virtual meetings.
